Passquare CARDPR

Here one arrives, as if by accident, and finds it was nothing of the sort. Self-made, as the saying goes, though one never quite knows whose saying it is: an international entrepreneur who wandered into loyalty, SaaS, and growth-hacking and found, rather curiously, that the whole world of business had been waiting there, quite as if it had been expecting company all along. Investing, building, crossing borders with the sort of ease that looks like luck until one notices it is not, and understanding how international affairs actually work, which is rarer than it ought to be. Open to partnerships, delighted to consult, and always ready for a curious conversation – preferably in one of the rooms below. A year of them sits there, rather more than one can decently attend alone. If you would like to go, write which event, and we shall see about going together.
